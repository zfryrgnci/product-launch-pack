Privacy Policy — Neon Blocks

Last updated: 2026. Applies to the Android app "Neon Blocks: Block Puzzle" (package com.refaz.neonblocks).

Information we collect

The app itself does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, precise location, contacts, or photos. Your score and game progress are stored locally on your device.

Ads and purchases

The app shows advertising through Google AdMob and offers a "Remove Ads" purchase through Google Play Billing. To provide these, Google's SDKs may process the device's Advertising ID and standard device/diagnostic information to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and process purchases. We never see your payment details.

Your choices

Where required, a consent screen lets you choose personalized vs. non‑personalized ads. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Buying the Remove Ads option removes advertising.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13.
