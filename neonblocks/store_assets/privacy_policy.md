# Privacy Policy — Neon Blocks

_Last updated: 2026_

This privacy policy applies to the **Neon Blocks** mobile game (the "App") for Android.

## Information the App collects
Neon Blocks itself does not ask you for any personal information and has no login.

The App shows advertising through **Google AdMob** and offers an optional in-app
purchase through **Google Play Billing**. To provide these services, Google's SDKs
may collect and process certain data, including:

- Advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) and device information used to
  serve and measure ads.
- Approximate, non-precise usage and diagnostics data.
- Purchase status for the "Remove Ads" product (handled entirely by Google Play;
  we never see your payment details).

We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, photos, or account credentials.

## How the data is used
Data collected by Google is used to display ads (personalized or non-personalized
depending on your consent), to prevent fraud, and to process your optional purchase.

## Your choices
- On first launch you are shown a consent screen (where required by law) letting you
  choose personalized vs. non-personalized ads.
- You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in **Android Settings → Privacy → Ads**.
- Purchasing "Remove Ads" disables all advertising in the App.

## Children
This App is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided
personal data, contact us and we will address it.
