## Full description
Drag. Fill. BLAST! NEON BLOCKS is the classic 8x8 block puzzle everyone plays —
reborn in glowing neon with juicy explosions, streak combos and buttery drag controls.

Place the 3 pieces anywhere on the board. Fill a full row or column and it BLASTS
away in a shower of neon. No timer, no pressure — but one wrong move and the board
locks up. How long can you keep it alive?

FEATURES
• Classic 8x8 block puzzle — learn it in 5 seconds, master it forever
• No time limit. Pure brain-teasing relaxation
• Juicy neon visuals: glowing blocks, particle blasts, screen shake
• STREAK system — chain clears back-to-back for up to x5 score multipliers
• Double and triple line blasts for massive bonus points
• Smart piece dealing that always gives you a fighting chance
• Ghost preview shows exactly where your piece lands and which lines will clear
• Offline — play anywhere, no internet needed
• Free forever

Your best score is saved. Beat it. Then beat it again.
